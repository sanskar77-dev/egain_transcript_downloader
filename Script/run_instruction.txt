Transcript Download
--------------------
Just change the month's dates below. Example — August 2026:

Why these times: 1 Aug 00:00:00 IST = 31 Jul 18:30:00 UTC, and
31 Aug 23:59:59.999 IST = 31 Aug 18:29:59.999 UTC. Using this instead of a
naive 2026-08-01T00:00:00.000Z to 2026-08-31T23:59:59.000Z avoids clipping
the last ~5.5 hours of July 31 evening (IST) sessions off the front, and
avoids pulling in the first ~5.5 hours of September 1 (IST) sessions at
the back.

cd "/Users/srathod/Desktop/Script"
python3 egain_transcript_downloader.py \
    --start 2026-07-31T18:30:00.000Z \
    --end   2026-08-31T18:29:59.999Z \
    --out   ./transcripts_august \
    --token-file token.txt \
    --workers 5
